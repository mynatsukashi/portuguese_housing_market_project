# portuguese_housing_market_project
Housing challenges in Portugal region from 2008 to 2024

Data, from official sources like PORDATA and Kaggle ( more than 10 000 data extracted from AirBNB, Booking.com, and local websites), was cleaned, sorted and analyzed with Excel, PowerBI tools. Additional measures were added for more accuracy and creativity. 
Main topics were covered:
- How female and male salary differs in Portugal over mentioned period?
- Energy efficiency and price difference - how energy certificates affect housing prices?
- How affordability ration changes over the time? ( calculated with DAX)
- What are the most expensive/cheapiest districts of Portugal?
- During the emigration flows, how severe was the unemployment rate?
